Apartment 3, Block 9, College Farm Street is a bright and well presented one bedroom apartment located in the popular College Farm development. Offering comfortable and low-maintenance living, this property is an ideal choice for first time buyers, downsizers or investors.

The accommodation is thoughtfully laid out, featuring a spacious living and dining room with feature bay window and open fireplace creating a warm and inviting focal point for the lounge, a fully fitted kitchen and a generous double bedroom and bathroom. The apartment benefits from a convenient location within easy walk of Newbridge town center, local shops, Arrow rail service and motorway network.

Combining comfort, convenience and affordability, this attractive one-bedroom apartment represents an excellent opportunity to secure a quality home in a well-established residential area.
